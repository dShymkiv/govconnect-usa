Privacy Policy

GovConnect USA — academic prototype by Diana Shymkiv (Northeastern Illinois University).

Last updated: November 22, 2026

What we collect

  • Account details you provide (name, email, mobile phone number)
  • Authentication data (password hash, SMS one-time codes)
  • Service activity needed to run the demo (wallet documents, appointment requests)

How we use SMS

We send transactional SMS only: login verification codes and optional appointment confirmations. We do not sell phone numbers or send marketing texts.

Opt-in / opt-out

By entering your phone number and requesting a verification code on the sign-in or registration form, you consent to receive SMS for that purpose. Reply STOP to opt out of further messages; reply HELP for help.
